The New Church Project

Building a house of worship.

A new permanent home for the RFM congregation — a house of prayer, a school of the Word, and a launching pad for missions. Rising by faith. Built together.

For years, RFM has met in rented spaces — faithful, growing, but longing for a permanent home. The New Church Project is our step of faith to build that home: a sanctuary for worship, classrooms for discipleship, a kitchen for hospitality, and offices for pastoral care.

What we are building

  • A main sanctuary seating 800+ people
  • Discipleship classrooms and library
  • Childrens ministry wing with age-appropriate rooms
  • Culinary ministry kitchen and dining hall
  • Prayer chapel — open 24/7
  • Pastoral offices and counseling rooms
