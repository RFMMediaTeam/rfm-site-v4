Our Mission

Serving God, serving people.

To share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, disciple believers, strengthen families, and serve communities through faith, compassion, and leadership development.

Pillar One

Preach the Gospel.

Faithful, Bible-centered preaching that calls every person to salvation and transformation in Christ. From Sunday pulpit to street-corner witness.

Pillar Two

Make disciples.

Small groups, Bible studies, mentorship, and leadership training. Deep roots so that fruit lasts, generation after generation.

Pillar Three

Serve the community.

Medical camps, food distribution, counseling, and outreach. Practical love in action — because faith without works, as James reminds us, is dead.
