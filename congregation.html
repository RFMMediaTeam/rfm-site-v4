Congregation

The gathered people of God.

The Church is not a building. It is a family — messy, growing, praying, worshipping. This is who we are together.

Our congregation gathers every Sunday morning at 11:15 AM at ACTC, Kavadiguda, Hyderabad. We come as we are — students, families, professionals, seekers, saints — and we leave sent by the Word, strengthened by worship, and knit together in prayer.

What a Sunday looks like

  • Worship — heartfelt praise led by our worship team.
  • Prayer — corporate intercession for the church, city, and nation.
  • Word — expository, life-giving preaching.
  • Fellowship — coffee, conversation, and community after service.

Life beyond Sunday

Sunday is only the start. Throughout the week, our congregation gathers in small groups, prayer meetings, mens and womens fellowships, youth nights, and outreach events. Church happens whenever two or three gather in His name.

They devoted themselves to the apostles' teaching and to fellowship, to the breaking of bread and to prayer. — Acts 2:42
