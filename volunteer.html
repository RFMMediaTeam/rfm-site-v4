Volunteer

Serve with us.

There is a place for you. Whatever your gift, your time, your season — we would be honored to serve alongside you.

Where you might serve

  • Worship & music — vocals, instruments, sound, lyrics.
  • Media & production — video, photography, streaming, social media.
  • Hospitality & ushering — welcoming guests, seating, coffee.
  • Childrens ministry — teaching, storytelling, activities.
  • Youth ministry — mentorship, event coordination.
  • Prayer team — Sunday altar prayer, intercession.
  • Culinary ministry — cooking, serving, hospitality meals.
  • Outreach — evangelism, community service, medical camps.
  • Administration — office, communications, event planning.
