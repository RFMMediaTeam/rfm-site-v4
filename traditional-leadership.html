Traditional Leadership

Leadership rooted in Scripture.

We believe leadership in the Church is not a corporate model but a biblical calling — grounded in the fivefold ministry, ordered by God's Word, and marked by servanthood.

Foundation

Classical

Rooted in the historic teachings of the Church — the creeds, the councils, the confessions that have carried the faith through centuries.

Framework

Systematic

Ordered, expository teaching of Scripture — book by book, doctrine by doctrine, so believers are grounded in the whole counsel of God.

Power

Charismatic

Open to the gifts and power of the Holy Spirit — prayer, prophecy, healing, and the fruit of the Spirit made visible in the community.

At Raja Faith Ministries, leadership is a sacred trust. We honor the traditional biblical pattern of apostles, prophets, evangelists, pastors, and teachers — each gift given by Christ to equip His people for the work of ministry.

The fivefold ministry

Ephesians 4 gives us a clear framework — leaders are given to build up, not to control; to release, not to restrict; to serve, not to be served. We hold to this pattern with humility and joy.

Pastoral care

Every member matters. Our pastoral team walks alongside families, visits homes, prays over the sick, counsels the hurting, and disciples the growing. Leadership is measured not by platform but by faithfulness in the small things.
