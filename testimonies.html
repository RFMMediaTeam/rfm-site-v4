Stories of grace.

God is still writing stories. Read what He has done in the lives of our RFM family — and share what He has done in yours.

Healing

"He restored what I thought was lost forever."

Years of prayer, tears, and waiting — and one Sunday, God moved. My marriage was restored, my faith renewed, my heart softened. RFM became my family.

— Sister P., Hyderabad

Salvation

"I walked in a skeptic. I left knowing Jesus."

A friend invited me — I came just to observe. That morning, the preaching cut through every wall. I gave my life to Christ that Sunday and have never looked back.

— Brother D., Secunderabad

Provision

"God provided when nothing else would."

Job lost, no savings, three kids. The church prayed, the culinary ministry brought meals, and within weeks I had a better job than before. God is faithful.

— Sister L., Kavadiguda

Freedom

"Ten years of addiction — gone in one prayer."

I thought I was too far gone. The prayer team at RFM stood with me, and Jesus set me free. Two years clean, serving in the men fellowship, telling everyone what He did.

— Brother K., Hyderabad
