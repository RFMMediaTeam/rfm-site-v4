Women Ministry

Faith • Strength • Sisterhood.

A gathering of women pursuing Christ together — through prayer, the Word, mentorship, and mission.

The Women Ministry at RFM is a home for women in every season — students, working professionals, homemakers, mothers, grandmothers. Together we grow deep in the Word, wide in prayer, and warm in fellowship.

What we do

  • Weekly fellowship — worship, testimony, shared Bible study
  • Prayer chain — an always-on intercession network
  • Mentorship — older women walking with younger women
  • Retreats and mission trips
  • Marriage & motherhood workshops
