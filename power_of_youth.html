Power of Youth

The rising generation.

A movement of young hearts on fire for Jesus — worship nights, discipleship, leadership training, and mission.

The youth are not the church of tomorrow — they are the church of today. Our youth ministry raises up a generation that knows the Word, walks in the Spirit, and takes bold steps to change their world.

What we do

  • Monthly worship nights — praise, prayer, prophetic ministry
  • Weekly Bible study and small groups
  • Leadership training and mentorship
  • Outreach and mission trips
  • Retreats and youth conferences
