Christian Nature Children

A joyful, safe, Christ-centered space for kids.

Where children learn the Bible through stories, songs, crafts, and play — and discover that God loves them personally.

Our children ministry is where the youngest hearts meet the biggest love. Every Sunday, kids gather for age-appropriate Bible teaching, worship, memory verses, and joyful activities led by our trained, safeguarded volunteer team.

Age groups

  • Little Lambs (Ages 2–5) — songs, stories, and simple prayers.
  • Little Disciples (Ages 6–9) — Bible characters, games, memory verses.
  • Junior Church (Ages 10–12) — deeper Bible study, application, and outreach.

Special events

Throughout the year, we host Vacation Bible School, Christmas plays, family days, and outdoor camps — bringing children and their parents together in celebration of Jesus.
