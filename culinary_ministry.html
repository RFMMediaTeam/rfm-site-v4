Culinary Ministry

Fed body, fed soul.

Every meal is a ministry. Every plate a prayer. Our culinary team turns kitchens into altars and dining tables into places of fellowship, healing, and welcome.

From the loaves and fishes to the Last Supper to the wedding feast of the Lamb — Scripture is full of food. The culinary ministry at RFM is a hands-on expression of hospitality, service, and love.

What we do

  • Sunday fellowship meals — bringing the family together around the table.
  • Meals of mercy — hot food for those grieving, sick, or in need.
  • Outreach feeding — hot meals and food packets for the poor and marginalized.
  • Conference & event catering — feeding hundreds during retreats, anniversaries, and camps.
  • Hospitality for guests — welcoming visiting pastors, missionaries, and families.

Join the kitchen

You do not have to be a chef. You just have to be willing. Whether you can chop, cook, plate, serve, or simply wash dishes — there is a place for you.
